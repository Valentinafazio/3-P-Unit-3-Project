Recipe #1 Greek Yogurt Cookie Dough

Ingredients Quantity
Vanilla Greek Yogurt 1 cup
Maple Syrup 3 Tabelespoons
Vanilla Extract 1 Teaspoon
Almond Flour 1 3/4 cups
Vanilla Protein Powder 1 Scoop
Almond Flour 1 3/4 cups
Chocolate Chips As many as you want!

Step 1: Get all your measuering tools ready!

Step 2:Add your yogurt and all your liquids together.

Step 3:Mix all the liquid ingredients together.

Step 4:Now add all your dry ingredients into the bowl.

Step 5:Mix both the dry and wet ingredients together.

Step 6:Add your prefered amount of chocolate chips and your done!

Recipe #2 Oreo Chocolate Parfait

Ingredients Quantity
Vanilla Greek Yogurt 10.6 oz
Sugar free chocolate pudding 1 pudding cup
Crushed oreo cookies 1-2 oreos
Cool whip 2 Tablespoons

Step 1: Layer the Greek yogurt in a glass.

Step 2: Add the chocolate pudding on top.

Step 3: Add the crushed oreo cookies on top.

Step 4: Add the cool whip on top.
